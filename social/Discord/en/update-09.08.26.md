## Alliance profile

Both changes are in the **Battle for Atlantis ranking** section, which shows how the alliance performed during the last 10 Battle for Atlantis events.

- Added actual vs expected victory points share to every past event. Previously only the ongoing event showed it, so you can now see how an alliance over- or underperformed across its whole recent history.
- Added a dedicated **division page**. Clicking any ranking card opens it, and it shows all 4 alliances taking part in that division together with their stats.
